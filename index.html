Ocean gap-filling methods explorer

This living bibliography was designed to help researchers compare gap-filling techniques for satellite, HF-radar, and in-situ data, with a focus on the open-source codes.

How to use it?

  1. Switch between datasets using the tabs below.
  2. Filter by method family, open-source availability, citation count, and publication year.
  3. Access the publications through their DOI.
